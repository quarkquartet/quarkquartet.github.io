I am a postdoctoral researcher at the [Institute for Fundamental Theory](https://ift.phys.ufl.edu/) at the University of Florida.
My research focuses on theoretical particle physics and cosmology.
My interests span baryogenesis, electroweak and Higgs physics, cosmological phase transitions, cosmic probes of light particles, and axion cosmology.

Before joining the University of Florida, I was a postdoctoral researcher in the Theory Division at Fermilab from October 2023 to September 2026.
I received my Ph.D. in theoretical particle physics from Rutgers University in 2023 under the supervision of David Shih and co-adsived by Keisuke Harigaya.
I completed my undergraduate studies in physics at Fudan University in Shanghai in 2017.

Outside research, I enjoy music and sports.
As an undergraduate, I served as associate concertmaster and principal second violin in University’s symphony orchestra.
While at Fermilab, I swam with the Masters team at [FMC Natatorium](https://www.fmcnatatorium.org/).

## Research Interests

- Baryogenesis
- Electroweak and Higgs physics
- Cosmological phase transitions
- Cosmological and astrophysical probes of light particles
- Axion cosmology
